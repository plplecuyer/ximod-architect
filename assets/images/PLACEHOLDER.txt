# Splash Image

Place your `splash.png` file here.

Recommended specifications:
- Format: PNG with transparency (alpha channel)
- Size: Up to 1024×1024 pixels (will be scaled to ~26% of screen width)
- Content: Application logo or branding image

The splash screen will:
1. Display centered on the primary screen
2. Show for the duration specified in Config.ini (SplashScreenSeconds)
3. Fade out smoothly over 500ms
4. Support full transparency on all platforms
